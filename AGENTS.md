# Website project instructions

## Purpose
This is Eric Laine's personal professional website. Its primary audience is recruiters, hiring managers, and thoughtful collaborators.

## Content rules
- Use LinkedIn as the source of truth for current public employment titles, dates, and achievements.
- Preserve Eric's voice: warm, capable, grounded, and specific. Avoid inflated claims and generic career language.
- Do not publish private career decisions, personal/family details, health information, financial information, or confidential employer information.
- Do not add metrics or claims unless they are present in an approved source or explicitly confirmed by Eric.
- Keep the site focused on renewals, customer growth, trusted relationships, sales/client onboarding, education, and cross-cultural leadership.

## Design rules
- Favor editorial clarity over decorative effects.
- Keep the site responsive, keyboard-accessible, and readable with reduced motion enabled.
- Use supplied assets locally when possible; do not hotlink personal images from LinkedIn.
- Prefer semantic HTML, CSS variables, and minimal JavaScript.

## Delivery rules
- Work on a feature branch; do not edit `main` directly.
- Run local verification before reporting completion.
- Do not merge, deploy, or make LinkedIn changes without explicit approval for that specific action.
